Food Trials</h3><h4>(water, applesauce and cookie)</h4><div>Position patient for food trials: hips at 90 degrees; head in midline; glasses on; dentures cleaned and in; hearing aid in and on.</div><div><strong>Perform oral care before and after food trials*</strong></div><div>Give in the following order and in the amounts specified.</div><ol><li>Sips of water to moisten mouth</li><li>90 mL water (1/2 of #77 Dixie cup)</li><li>Three tsp. applesauce (1 tsp. each time)</li><li>Cookie</li></ol><div>Observe for:</div><ul><li>Coughing / choking</li><li>Change in voice quality (becomes wet/gurgly)</li><li>Ability to swallow (absent/weak/delayed/effortful)</li></ul></section><section class="section"><table><tr><td>If patient can manage all consistencies:</td><td><ul><li>Order DAT</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>If patient can only manage applesauce:</td><td><ul><li>Order purees with thickened fluids</li><li>Crush meds</li><li>Refer to S-LP/OT and RD</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>If patient is unable to manage any consistency:</td><td><ul><li>Initiate NPO</li><li>Refer to S-LP/OT and RD</li></ul></td></tr></table></section><section class="section"><div>For patients placed on a diet,<strong>monitor initials feeds closely.</strong><span>&nbsp; Closely observe swallowing ability and behaviours at meal times.</span></div><div><strong>Monitor for signs of aspiration:</strong><span>&nbsp; Wet/Gurgly voice quality; frequent coughing; deterioration in respiratory status.</span></div><div><strong>Unless other medical reasons prevail, maintain head of bed at 30 degrees, even during sleep*</strong></div><div><strong>* both are factors in preventing aspiration pneumonia</strong></div></section><section class="section viewOnlyHidden"><div>Email &nbsp;<a href="mailto:info@edehr.org?subject=Call me&amp;body=dummy mail:">info@edehr.org</a><span>&nbsp; to provide feedback or report concerns regarding this order set.</span></div></section>
